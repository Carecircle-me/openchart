# Royalty-free patent commitment

Modelled on the Open Web Foundation Final Specification Agreement (OWFa 1.0). This plain-language commitment is the operative text for this project.

## 1. Commitment by CareCircle Connect Inc.

CareCircle Connect Inc. ("CareCircle") hereby makes the following irrevocable (except as stated in section 4), worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive commitment:

CareCircle will not assert, and grants every implementer a licence under, any patent claims that CareCircle owns or controls now or in the future and that are **necessarily infringed** by implementing any **released version** of the PDRE (Patient-Directed Record Exchange) specification published in this repository (the "Specification"), to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale, import and distribute implementations of the Specification.

"Necessarily infringed" means there is no commercially reasonable, non-infringing way to implement the relevant normative part of the Specification.

## 2. Who it covers

Anyone, for any purpose, commercial or not, open source or closed source. You do not need to sign anything, register, pay anything, or use any CareCircle product or service to benefit from this commitment.

## 3. What it covers and doesn't

- Covers: the normative parts of each released version of the Specification (including draft versions published in this repository).
- Does not cover: enabling technologies that the Specification only references (for example HL7 FHIR, OAuth, TLS or DICOM), parts of an implementation that are not required by the Specification, or trademarks (see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)).
- Code in this repository is also covered by the patent grant in the Apache License 2.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE), section 3).

## 4. Defensive termination

If you, or anyone acting for you, sue CareCircle or any implementer alleging that an implementation of the Specification infringes a patent, this commitment ends for you as of the date you filed that claim.

## 5. Contributors

Everyone who contributes text to the Specification makes the same commitment for their own necessary patent claims in their contribution, as a condition of contributing (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## 6. No other rights

This commitment grants no rights other than those stated here. It is provided without warranty (see [DISCLAIMER.md](DISCLAIMER.md)).
