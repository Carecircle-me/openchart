# Governance

## Today: founder-led, open by default

CareCircle Connect Inc. is the steward and initial maintainer of OpenChart, its PDRE protocol specification and this repository. Everything is developed in public: issues, RFCs, decisions and releases.

## Our commitments

1. **The protocol stays open.** The specification stays under CC BY 4.0, the code under Apache-2.0, and the royalty-free patent commitment in [PATENTS.md](PATENTS.md) is irrevocable for released drafts.
2. **No lock-in.** Implementing PDRE never requires using any CareCircle product or service. Anyone can implement it and anyone can run a compatible hub.
3. **Equal treatment.** CareCircle's own products get no private APIs, early spec access or certification shortcuts. Directory listings have no paid placement.
4. **Neutral home.** Once PDRE has two or more independent implementations, we will form a steering committee in which CareCircle holds a seat but no veto. Over time we intend to move the PDRE specification to a neutral body (for example HL7 International or a Canadian not-for-profit foundation), together with the neutral protocol name.

## Phases

| Phase | When | Decision makers |
|---|---|---|
| A. Founder-led | Now (v0.x) | Maintainers at CareCircle Connect Inc., after public comment |
| B. Steering committee | After 2+ independent implementations | Rotating seats for vendors (no single company with a majority), clinicians by discipline, patient representatives, privacy/security experts and hub operators |
| C. Neutral home | When adoption justifies it | The receiving foundation or standards body |

## How decisions are made

- Day-to-day code changes: maintainer review and merge.
- Spec changes: the RFC process in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), with public comment, then a maintainer decision recorded with its reasons.
- In phase B: lazy consensus, with a two-thirds vote as a fallback. Changes that benefit CareCircle products also need a majority of non-CareCircle members.

## Maintainers

Initial maintainers are from CareCircle Connect Inc. Contributors who show sustained, high-quality work can be invited to become maintainers.
